# The activity log

## title
A short guide to the activity log, the record of every change.

## opening
The activity log records who changed what, and when.

## f-activity-list
Each change shows when it happened, what changed and who made it.

## f-activity-filter
Filter by kind, such as risks and issues. Or by the person who made the change.

## closing
When a date or an owner changes without notice, the log shows who did it, and when.
