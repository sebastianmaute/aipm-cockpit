# RACI

## title
A short guide to the RACI matrix: who does what for each milestone.

## opening
RACI makes clear who is responsible, accountable, consulted and informed for each milestone, a key date of the project. Nobody has to guess.

## f-raci-matrix
Responsible does it, accountable answers for it, consulted advises, informed is told.

## f-raci-cell
Set one cell. Elena Fischer becomes accountable for the kickoff.

## closing
When each milestone has one person who answers for it, decisions do not fall through the gaps.
