# Tune next actions with AI

## title
A short guide to letting Claude tune how next actions are ranked.

## opening
Next actions are ranked by weights, how much each kind of warning counts. Claude tunes them from what you acted on.

## ai-weights-suggest
In Settings, Suggest with AI reads which warnings you acted on, and which you snoozed or dismissed over the past weeks. Claude proposes new weights.

## ai-weights-accept
You accept the one you agree with, and the ranking follows it.

## closing
The list learns what you treat as urgent, so it spends less of your attention on what you always put off.
