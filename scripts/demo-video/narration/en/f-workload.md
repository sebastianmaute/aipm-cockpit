# Workload

## title
A short guide to the workload view, which shows who has too much to do.

## opening
Workload shows how busy each person is over time, so you can rebalance before anyone is overloaded.

## f-workload-over
Maya Patel is booked far over her capacity, the time she actually has.

## f-workload-reassign
Reassign one of her overdue tasks. Here, it goes to Liam Foster.

## f-workload-counts
The counts follow the task: Maya has one open and one overdue task fewer, Liam one more of each.

## f-workload-rebalance
Util now is the time planned for each person, not the tasks. So lower Maya's planned time, and give Liam more.

## closing
Moving work early is easier than explaining a missed date later.
