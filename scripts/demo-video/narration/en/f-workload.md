# Workload

## title
A short guide to the workload view, which shows who has too much to do.

## opening
Workload shows how busy each person is over time, so you can rebalance before anyone is overloaded.

## f-workload-over
Maya Patel is booked far over her capacity, the time she actually has.

## f-workload-reassign

## closing
