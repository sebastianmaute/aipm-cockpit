# Version history

## title
A short guide to version history, the saved states of a project.

## opening
Version history keeps every saved state of the project. You can compare two, and restore one.

## f-history-list
History lists every saved version of the project, the newest first.

## f-history-compare
Pick two and compare them, to see what changed in between.

## closing
A saved version lets you show what the plan was, not only what it is now.
