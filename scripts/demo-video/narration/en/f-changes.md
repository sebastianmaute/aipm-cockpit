# Changes

## title
A short guide to the change log, where changes to scope, time and budget are agreed.

## opening
The change log records agreed changes to scope, time or budget. That keeps the baseline, the plan you agreed at the start, honest.

## f-changes-open
Open a change request. This one adds MFA, a second login step, to the scope.

## f-changes-impact
Its impact: a rating, and what it does to scope, time and cost.

## f-changes-status
Its status follows it through review to a decision. Here, it is approved.

## closing
When every change is written down and decided, nobody argues later about what was agreed.
