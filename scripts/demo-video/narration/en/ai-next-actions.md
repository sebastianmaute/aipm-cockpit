# Analyze next actions with AI

## title
A short guide to asking Claude what needs your attention first.

## opening
Next actions lists every warning in the project, sorted by urgency. Analyze with AI explains what needs you first, and why.

## mon-analyze
Claude reads the whole list and picks the few items that matter most today, each with a short reason. You read it, then close it and work from the list.

## closing
A long list of warnings is easier to act on once you know where to start.
