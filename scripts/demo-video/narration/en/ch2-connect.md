# Get started - connect and create a project

## title
Getting started takes two connections: a database for your projects, and Claude, the AI.

## setup-first-screen
On a fresh install, the start screen offers three ways in. Each of them needs the connections first.

## setup-connect
Set up Turso first opens the setup, a few short steps.

## setup-turso
Turso is an online database that keeps your projects. You paste its address and access token, test the connection, and keep your portfolio there, the list of all your projects.

## setup-key
The key lets the app use Claude. It is stored encrypted, on this computer only.

## setup-model
Once the key works, the list of Claude models fills in. These videos use Sonnet 5.5.

## setup-jira-optional
Jira, a work tracker many teams use, can be linked later.

## setup-review
The last page lists what is connected. Finish saves the setup, and the app reloads on the new database.

## prologue-describe
A new project starts from a short description: what it is for, who it serves, how many people, and when. Generate with AI hands it to Claude, which proposes the setup for you to check.

## first-gdpr
GDPR is the EU's data protection law. The brief mentions deleting customer data, so Claude ticks it.

## first-template
A template brings typical tasks to start from. Empty starts with a blank plan.

## first-functions
Functions are the parts of the app, such as Budget or Stakeholders. Turn on the ones this project uses. You can change that later.

## first-project-lands
The project now exists, saved to the database, empty and ready for its first tasks.

## first-open-demo
To see a project in full swing, you open the demo from Projects. It is a complete sample, with a team, tasks, risks and a budget. The app also switches on two things the next chapters use: the weekly status report and the AI Assistant.

## demo-opens
The demo is a customer identity platform for Northwind, a retailer: how its customers sign up and sign in.

## closing
Its MVP, the first working version, was due last Friday and missed its date. The next chapters follow the project lead through the week that puts it right, from Monday to Friday.
