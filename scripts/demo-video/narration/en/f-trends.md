# Trends

## title
A short guide to trends: how a project moves from week to week.

## opening
Trends compare today with a baseline, a saved state of the plan you measure against. You see whether schedule and budget are drifting.

## f-trends-weekly
A snapshot is saved every week, so you see how the project moved.

## f-trends-baseline
Pick a week as the baseline to compare against.

## closing
Drift is hard to see from one day to the next, and easy to see across weeks.
