# The resource directory

## title
A short guide to the resource directory, the list of people on a project.

## opening
Resources are the people who do the work. Track their roles, their capacity, which is the time they have, and their time off, so you plan realistically.

## f-directory-list
The directory lists everyone who works on the project.

## f-directory-person
Open a person to see their role, contact details and working time.

## f-directory-roles
Roles carry the rates, internal or external, per hour or per day.

## closing
Knowing who is available, and at what rate, is the base of every plan and every budget.
