# Insight recommendations

## title
A short guide to turning an insight into a step you can take.

## opening
Insights are patterns the app finds in your project, such as spending that runs off plan. Each one can get a recommendation.

## fri-insight-rec
For the budget insight, Generate recommendation asks Claude what to do about it. The suggestion appears on the card.

## fri-insight-apply
Apply recommendation first shows the changes it proposes. Once you have checked them, Apply makes them in the project.

## closing
A pattern only helps once it turns into a step that someone takes.
