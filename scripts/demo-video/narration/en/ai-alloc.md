# Plan allocations with AI

## title
A short guide to planning who works how much, with Claude's help.

## opening
Plan with AI proposes allocations: who works on the project how much, and when.

## ai-alloc-over
Maya Patel is booked far over 100 percent in September, more hours than she has. You ask Plan with AI, in plain words, to bring her under 100 percent by sharing her work with the team. Claude proposes a new split.

## ai-alloc-workload
You apply it, and Workload shows the new split, person by person.

## closing
An overbooked person is cheaper to spot in the plan than in a missed date.
