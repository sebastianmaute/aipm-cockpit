# Wednesday - people

## title
Wednesday is about people: who does the new work, who is away, and who has too much on their plate.

## wed-discipline-role
The CRM connector needs a specialist. A discipline is the kind of work. The role adds a level, here Senior, and its hourly rates, internal and external.

## wed-new-member
Lukas Brandt joins from the vendor's partner firm. He goes into the directory, the project's list of people, with the new role.

## wed-noah-vacation
Noah Bennett owns the CRM integration. He is away next week, so you log the absence. It then shows in the plan and in his workload.

## wed-maya-util
Workload compares each person's planned hours with the time they have. Maya Patel is over-allocated this month, so you bring her hours down.

## wed-planning-member
Planning books each person's time, month by month. Lukas starts at 40 percent in September.

## wed-plan-with-ai
For the rest, you tell Claude what you want: Lukas on the connector, 80 percent in October. It proposes the allocation, how his time is split across the months, and you apply it.

## wed-swimlane
Swimlanes split the board into one row per person. Lukas gets a lane, and his first task, a CRM follow-up, moves into it.

## closing
That is Wednesday. The new role exists, Lukas is on the team and planned, Noah's absence is known, and Maya's load fits her hours again. Tomorrow: risks, stakeholders and money.
