# Deduplicate and unify

## title
A short guide to finding and merging duplicate tasks with Claude.

## opening
Deduplicate and unify finds tasks that say the same thing in different words, and merges them.

## tue-dedup
Two people logged the same follow-ups. Claude compares the open tasks and lists each likely pair. You keep the merges you agree with, and Merge selected turns each pair into one task.

## closing
One task for each piece of work means nobody does it twice, and your counts stay honest.
