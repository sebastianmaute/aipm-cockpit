# Optimize with AI

## title
A short guide to restructuring open tasks with Optimize with AI.

## opening
Optimize with AI turns a loose list of open tasks into a work breakdown structure: the work split into pieces small enough to plan.

## fri-optimize-wbs
You tell Claude what you want: split anything longer than a week, group the pilot and rollout tasks, and link what depends on what. Claude proposes the splits, groups, estimates and links. You review them, then apply.

## closing
Small, linked tasks are easier to estimate and assign, and the schedule shows what waits on what.
