# Absences

## title
A short guide to logging absences, such as vacation or training.

## opening
Log vacation, training and other time off. It then shows on the Gantt timeline and in workload, the view of how busy each person is.

## f-absences-log
Log an absence: who it is, what kind, and from when to when. Here, Liam Foster takes a week of vacation.

## f-absences-calendar
It shows in the calendar, next to the team meetings.

## closing
When time off is on the plan, you do not give work to someone who is away.
