# Set up a project with Claude

## title
A short guide to starting a new project with Claude's help.

## opening
You describe the project in one sentence. Claude proposes the setup, and you check it.

## prologue-describe
New project opens the setup wizard. You type a short brief: a customer identity platform for an online retailer, five people, March to February. Generate with AI sends it to Claude, which drafts the name, the dates and the key facts.

## prologue-gdpr
The brief mentions deleting customer data, so Claude ticks GDPR, the European data protection law. The template and the functions come next.

## prologue-cancel
The last step creates it. Here, you cancel and stay in the demo.

## closing
A first draft from one sentence spares you a dozen empty fields, and you still decide what stays.
