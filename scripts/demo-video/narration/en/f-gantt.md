# The Gantt chart

## title
A short guide to planning a schedule with the Gantt chart.

## opening
The Gantt chart shows tasks and milestones, your key dates, on a timeline. Link dependencies to see which task must finish before another can start.

## tue-dedup
First, Deduplicate and unify. Claude finds tasks that were logged twice, and you merge each pair into one.

## f-gantt-drag-bar
Drag a bar, and its start and end move together.

## f-gantt-drag-end
Drag its right edge, and only the due date moves.

## f-gantt-drag-start
The left edge moves the start, which changes the duration.

## f-gantt-drag-milestone
Drag a milestone, and it moves to a later date.

## f-gantt-status-filter
The status filter hides what is already completed.

## f-gantt-view-dependencies
In the View menu, dependencies draw the links between tasks: what must finish before what.

## f-gantt-view-absences
Absences, such as vacation, show on the timeline, so you see who is away.

## f-gantt-view-critical-path
The critical path is the chain of tasks where any delay moves the end date.

## f-gantt-view-milestones
Milestones have a layer of their own.

## f-gantt-view-inline-milestones
Or show milestones inline, in the task rows.

## closing
A timeline shows you early when one late task will push back the tasks that depend on it.
