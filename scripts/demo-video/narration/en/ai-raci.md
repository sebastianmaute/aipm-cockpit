# Suggest RACI

## title
A short guide to filling in a RACI matrix with Claude.

## opening
A RACI matrix says, for each milestone, who is responsible, who is accountable, who is consulted and who is informed. Suggest RACI fills it from your milestones and people.

## thu-suggest-raci
Claude reads the milestones and the people, and proposes a letter for each empty cell. You can untick any that do not fit. Here, you apply them all.

## closing
Clear roles spare you the meeting where everyone assumed someone else would decide.
