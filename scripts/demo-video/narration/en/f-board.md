# The board

## title
A short guide to the board, which shows your tasks as cards, sorted by status.

## opening
The same tasks as a board. Drag a card to change its status.

## f-board-switch
Switch to Board, and you get one column per status.

## f-board-drag
Drag a card to the next column, and its status follows.

## closing
A board shows at a glance where work piles up.
