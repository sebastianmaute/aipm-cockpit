# Planning

## title
A short guide to capacity planning: how much time each person gives the project.

## opening
Planning sets how much time each person has for the project in each period. That is the base for a budget the team can actually deliver.

## f-planning-month
Plan how much of each month a person gives the project. Liam Foster gets sixty percent of October and eighty percent of November.

## closing
A plan that matches the hours people really have is a plan you can keep.
