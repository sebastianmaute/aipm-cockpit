# RAID

## title
A short guide to the RAID register, where a project tracks what could go wrong.

## opening
RAID logs your risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies: the things that could knock the project off course.

## f-raid-register
All four kinds sit in one register, with a filter by kind.

## f-raid-open
Open an issue, a problem that is already happening. Here, requests to delete customer data never reach the customer database.

## f-raid-link
Link it to the task that fixes it, so the problem and its fix stay together.

## closing
A problem that is written down, owned and linked to its fix does not get lost between meetings.
