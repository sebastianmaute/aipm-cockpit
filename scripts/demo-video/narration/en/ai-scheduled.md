# Scheduled AI jobs

## title
A short guide to scheduled AI jobs that check the project for you.

## opening
A scheduled job runs an AI check on the project at a set time, without you starting it.

## ai-scheduled-job
Here, a job called Morning check runs every day at nine for this project. You give it its own instruction, what to look for, and its time zone.

## ai-scheduled-runs
At nine, it starts by itself. Claude reads the project and follows the instruction.

## ai-scheduled-ran
It ran. The job shows when, and how many suggestions it found.

## ai-scheduled-report
Every run leaves a report under Scheduled reports. Open the newest to read what Claude found.

## closing
A check that runs every morning catches a problem even on a day you are too busy to look.
