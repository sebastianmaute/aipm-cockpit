# Draft the steering report

## title
A short guide to drafting a steering committee report with Claude.

## opening
The steering committee is the group that makes the big decisions on a project. Draft with AI writes its report from the project's current state.

## thu-steering-draft
You open the status report for the next meeting. Draft with AI fills it in from the tasks, risks and dates as they stand. You edit what you like, then save.

## closing
The report starts from facts already in the project, so your time goes into the message, not the copying.
