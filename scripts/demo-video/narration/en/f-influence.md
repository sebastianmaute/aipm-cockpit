# The stakeholder map

## title
A short guide to the stakeholder map, which sorts people by influence and interest.

## opening
Plot each stakeholder by influence and interest, then drag them between the four quadrants to agree how closely to manage them.

## f-influence-drag
Drag a stakeholder to another quadrant. Sam Rivera moves to manage closely: high influence, and high interest.

## closing
The map shows which people need most of your attention.
