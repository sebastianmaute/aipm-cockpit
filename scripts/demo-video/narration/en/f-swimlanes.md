# Swimlanes

## title
A short guide to swimlanes, the board split into rows.

## opening
Swimlanes split the board into lanes, horizontal rows that group the cards.

## f-swimlanes-lanes
Now each person has a lane of their own.

## f-swimlanes-hide-done
Hide finished work, and the lanes keep only what is still open.

## closing
Lanes show who carries too much, and who has room for more.
