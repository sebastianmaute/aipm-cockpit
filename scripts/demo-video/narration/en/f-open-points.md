# Open points

## title
A short guide to the open points list, where you track the tasks of a project.

## opening
Open points are the tasks that move the project forward. Give each one a status and a due date, so nothing stalls.

## f-open-points-add
Add a task with a name, an owner and a due date.

## f-open-points-sort
Click a column header to sort by it.

## f-open-points-filter
Filter the list to one person.

## f-open-points-status
Set the status right in the row.

## closing
When every task has an owner, a date and a status, nothing stalls without anyone noticing.
