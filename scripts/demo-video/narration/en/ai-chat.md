# The AI Assistant

## title
A short guide to the AI Assistant, the chat that knows your project.

## opening
You can ask the assistant anything about the project. It can also make the change for you.

## ai-chat-ask
You ask why the MVP target slipped, the first working version, and what needs doing this week. Claude reads the tasks, risks and dates, and answers in plain words.

## ai-chat-change
Or you ask it to make a change: a task to book the call with the CRM vendor, for Ava Thompson, due on 2 October.

## ai-chat-in-open-points
The task is in Open Points, the task list, with its owner and date.

## closing
One question in the chat is quicker than a search through five lists, and the change lands where the team works.
