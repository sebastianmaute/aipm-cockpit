# Milestones

## title
A short guide to milestones, the key dates a project commits to.

## opening
Milestones are the key dates you commit to. Track them to show whether the plan is on schedule.

## f-milestones-list
Each milestone shows its date and the work linked to it.

## f-milestones-add
Add a milestone. Here, a security sign-off, with its date.

## f-milestones-horizon
Across the top, the horizon sorts them into overdue, this week, the next two weeks, and later.

## closing
When you see the next dates side by side, you notice early which one is at risk.
