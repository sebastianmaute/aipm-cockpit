# Budget

## title
A short guide to the project budget: what you planned to spend, and what you spent.

## opening
The budget plans effort and cost by role and period. Compare it with what was actually spent, the actuals, to spot overspend early.

## f-budget-setup
It is built from role rates, people, a bucket of money per phase, and booked hours.

## f-budget-plan-actual
Plan against actual, for each bucket and for the whole project.

## closing
Comparing plan and actual every week shows overspend while there is still time to correct it.
