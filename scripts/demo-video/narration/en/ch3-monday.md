# Monday - a milestone slipped

## title
Monday morning. On Friday the team missed a milestone, a date the project had promised: the first working version, the MVP.

## mon-do-first
The dashboard puts that slipped date on top, before anything else.

## mon-since-friday
Underneath, what changed since Friday. RAID, the list of risks and issues, has a new entry, and one more task is late.

## week-digest
Generate now asks Claude for a short status report: where the project stands, what is late, and which risks are open. You read it in a minute instead of checking every list.

## mon-next-actions
Next actions gathers every warning in the project into one list, sorted by urgency. Now needs you first, soon comes next, and the last group is only to watch.

## mon-analyze
Analyze with AI reads the same list. Claude picks the few items that need you first and explains why, in plain words. You read it, then close it and go back to the list.

## mon-crm-blocker
This task is why the MVP is late. The CRM, the customer database, refuses requests to delete customer data. You note that as a blocker, visible to everyone, and move the due date to Thursday.

## mon-knowledge-crm
Knowledge keeps the team's decisions. A search for CRM finds the one made at design sign-off.

## mon-draft-elena
Elena Fischer is the sponsor. She funds the project and must hear about the slip. Draft message opens a ready-made update to her in your mail program, to check and send from there.

## mon-chat-elena
The other way is to let the assistant write it. A stakeholder is anyone the project affects or answers to. The Stakeholder update prompt has Claude draft the message from the project as it is today, slip included.

## mon-save-version
Last, you save a version, a snapshot of Monday's plan. On Friday you can compare the week against it.

## closing
That is Monday. The slip is in plain view, its cause is recorded, the sponsor is told, and the plan is saved. Tomorrow, you re-plan.
