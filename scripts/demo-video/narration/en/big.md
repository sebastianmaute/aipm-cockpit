# A week with the cockpit

## title
One project lead, one project, one week, from the first setup to Friday's review.

## opening
The project in this video missed its first big date on Friday. First, the app needs two connections: a database for your projects, and Claude, the AI.

## setup-model
Once the key works, the list of Claude models fills in. This video uses Sonnet 5.5.

## prologue-gdpr
Claude fills in the key facts. The brief mentions deleting customer data, so it ticks GDPR, the EU's data protection law.

## prologue-cancel
The last step would create the project. This time you cancel and work in the demo project instead. It is Northwind's customer identity platform: how the retailer's customers sign up and sign in. Its MVP, the first working version, was due last Friday and missed its date.

## mon-do-first
Monday morning. The dashboard puts the slipped MVP date on top, before anything else.

## week-digest
The weekly digest is a short status report from Claude. Generate now has it sum up where the project stands, what changed, and what is still open, so you need not check every list.

## tue-board-crm
Tuesday, the re-plan. The board shows tasks as cards in status columns, and the CRM task moves to In Progress.

## wed-discipline-role
Wednesday is about people. The connector needs a specialist: a new discipline, the kind of work, then a role with its level, here Senior, and its hourly rates, internal and external.

## thu-gdpr-owner
Thursday is about risks, stakeholders and money. In RAID, the GDPR issue, erasure requests that never reach the CRM, gets an owner, Noah Bennett, and a mitigation, the plan that fixes it. Work on it starts.

## thu-licence-risk
A risk is a problem that might still happen. The licence for the CRM connector could take a while to arrive, and Noah owns this one too.

## thu-dpo-stakeholder
Northwind's data protection officer, the DPO, watches over customer data. The DPO joins the stakeholders.

## thu-steering-approve
The steering committee is the small group of senior people who make the big decisions. At today's meeting it approves both change requests, the CRM connector and MFA. You set each one to Approved.

## thu-connector-bucket
The approved connector gets a budget bucket of its own, so its cost stays visible apart from the rest.

## fri-crm-done
Friday. Erasure works end to end: the CRM task is Done.

## fri-gdpr-resolved
In RAID, the GDPR issue is marked Resolved. Its cause is gone.

## fri-do-first
The dashboard's top item has changed. Now the first thing to do is tell the DPO, before the re-demo.

## fri-draft-dpo
Draft message again, this time for the DPO: the erasure fix is verified.

## fri-digest
The digest again, this time on Friday. Claude sums up the project as it stands now: the CRM fix done, the GDPR issue resolved, and the new dates in the plan.

## closing
That is the week. The cause of the slip is fixed and verified, the plan and the budget hold the new dates, the team's load fits its hours, and the sponsor and the DPO have heard. Each day also has a chapter video of its own.
