# Tuesday - re-plan

## title
Tuesday. Monday found why the MVP, the first working version, slipped. Today you re-plan around it.

## tue-board-crm
The board shows tasks as cards in status columns. The CRM task, the customer database link, goes to In Progress.

## tue-delete-duplicate
The same follow-up was logged twice on Friday. You open one copy and delete it.

## tue-dedup
Finding every pair by hand takes long. Deduplicate and unify has Claude find the tasks that say the same thing, and you merge each pair into one.

## tue-change-connector
A change request proposes a change to what the project delivers, with its cost, for someone to approve. This one swaps the failing CRM link for a ready-made connector.

## tue-chat-link
The assistant can change several items at once. You ask it to link the change request to the tasks it affects and to the GDPR issue, a problem that is already happening. Each task also gets a short note. Dates, owners and statuses stay as they are.

## tue-chat-linked
The links are in. The change request now lists everything it affects.

## tue-chat-review
Nothing is written yet. The card lists each edit. You check them, then apply all of them in one step.

## tue-draft-maya
Maya Patel waits for news on the MFA change, a second step at sign-in. Draft message opens your mail program with an update to her.

## tue-chat-maya
Or the assistant writes it. You ask for an update to Maya on the MFA change and on the new plan, and Claude drafts it from the project as it is today.

## tue-milestone-redemo
A milestone is a date the project promises. The customer demo gets its own: MVP re-demo, on 2 October. The demo task is linked to it and taken off the old MVP target.

## tue-demo-task-moves
The demo task's due date follows, to Friday 2 October, the day of the re-demo.

## tue-bucket-hours
A budget bucket is one pot of the budget. This one pays by the hour, so it plans hours per person.

## tue-follow-plan-off
Normally the budget's hours follow the plan. To bring in the team's own figures, you switch that off.

## tue-forecast-import
The team sent its re-plan as a spreadsheet: a forecast, their estimate of the hours still ahead. Claude reads the columns and matches them to the budget's fields. You look at the preview, then apply it.

## closing
That is Tuesday. Work on the CRM has restarted, the duplicates are gone, the change request is written up, the re-demo has a date, and the budget holds the new hours. Tomorrow is about people.
