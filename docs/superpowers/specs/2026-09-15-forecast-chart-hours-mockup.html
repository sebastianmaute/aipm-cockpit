MR 3 mockup — forecast lines in € and hours

Local throwaway mockup. Fixture = spec §5.6 (contract €240k, plan 5 Jan – 18 Dec 2026, today 14 Sep, monthly plan), extended with an hours side: budget 2,000 h at a planned average of €120/h.

Why the hours chart has no forecast today

  • Spec ruling, not a technical limit. §2 set the money basis to contract price (Employer Burndown standard), so MR 2 built € facts only.
  • The engine does not care about units. computeForecastFromFacts reads bac · ac · ev · pv · dated[].value and nothing else. Feed it hours and it returns burn rate h/day, ETC/EAC/VAC in hours, a run-out date, CPI/SPI in hours.
  • All inputs exist. Dated actual hours are already walked in forecastFacts; BAC/AC hours sit on report.project; EV hours = Σ bucket budget hours × % complete; PV hours = the chart's own hours series.
  • Hours are simpler. No rates, no FX, and no fixed-price uncapping (§5.4) — hours are the internal-effort truth.

Forecast cards — showing hours and signalling when the units disagree

Everything below recomputes from facts with the MR 2 formulas. Switch the scenario to see each signal appear, flip direction, or disappear.

Scenario

The mechanism, in one number

€ and hours only diverge when the average rate of the hours actually booked differs from the planned average rate (€240,000 ÷ 2,000 h = €120/h). Everything else — which forecast looks worse, by how much, and why — follows from that drift and from which roles caused it. So every signal below is driven by the same two derived facts:

  • Rate drift = (AC € ÷ AC h) ÷ (BAC € ÷ BAC h) − 1 →
  • Driver role = role whose share of booked hours exceeds its planned share the most →

Proposed trigger (open question): signal when |rate drift| ≥ 3% or the € and hours pace VAC fall into different RAG bands. Scenario 1 trips both; 2 trips the drift rule; 3 trips neither. Fixed-price buckets are excluded from the drift (their € actual is already an hours ratio, §5.4).

Card layouts

Card 1 — € card, hours as a second line recommended

€ stays the headline (matches RAG, tile, contract). One compact hours line per card. When the trigger trips, the line carries a text chip with a tooltip that explains why.

Card 2 — two value columns, disagreeing rows highlighted

Every figure in € and h side by side. The row where the story differs gets a tinted background and an arrow — readable, but twice the numbers on every visit, even when nothing diverges.

Card 3 — unit switch on the section (shares the chart's € | h setting)

Cards show one unit at a time. Risk this mockup exists to expose: a user who never switches never sees the hours story — so it only works together with a banner that offers the switch.

Signals — candidates to make the divergence transparent

Not mutually exclusive. The recommendation at the end combines a trigger-gated banner (S1) with the explanation (S4) and a permanent small fact (S2).

S1 — Banner at the top of the Forecast section (trigger-gated) recommended

Shared Banner, stacks with the §6.1a banners, role="status", starts with a word so the state is not colour-only. Wording flips with the direction. Absent when the trigger does not trip.

S2 — Rate fact in the facts row (always visible)

A fifth fact beside BAC · AC · Remaining · EV. Quiet when on plan, carries an arrow + text when drifting. Teaches the mechanism before anything goes wrong.

S3 — Three bars on one scale: hours used · budget used · work complete

Visual: the gap between the first two bars is the mix effect; the gap to the third is the efficiency problem. No new terms needed.

S4 — "Where the hours went": role mix disclosure recommended

The cause, not just the flag. Collapsed <details> under the banner (or opened from its action). Data exists: bucket rate rows carry role, rate, budget and actual hours.

S5 — Dashboard tile: hours chip under the € headline

The tile cannot hold a banner. One text chip, only when the trigger trips; its tooltip carries the one-sentence explanation and links to the Budget report.

Chip text names the unit and the figure, so it is not colour-only. Gone in scenario 3.

S6 — Tooltip on the € VAC (and on the chip)

Weakest alone (hover/focus only, invisible on touch until tapped) but cheap and useful as the second layer behind S1/S5. Hover the ⓘ in Card 1 to see the text.

Recommendation to discuss

  • Cards: Card 1 — € headline card + one hours line per card (EAC h · VAC h · runs out).
  • Signal: S1 banner when the trigger trips, its action opens S4 (role mix); S2 rate fact always in the facts row; S5 chip on the tile; S6 tooltip text shared by chip and hours line.
  • Chart: A (unit switch) — or C (% view) if the banner should never need a click to be verified.
  • Unchanged: RAG and tile headline stay €. Hours never change a rating; they explain it.
  • Open: trigger thresholds (3% drift / RAG band), name of the hours CPI if Card 2 wins ("CPI (hours)" beside Effort CPI), whether S4 belongs to MR 3 or a follow-up.
