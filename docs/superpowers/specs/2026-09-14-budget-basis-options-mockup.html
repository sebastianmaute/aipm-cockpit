Budget basis: option 1 vs option 2

Two ways to show the executive figures (§499), EVM wording (§501) and a burn-down forecast (§504) in contract money. Same illustrative project in both: contract budget €240,000, planned 5 Jan – 18 Dec 2026, today 14 Sep 2026, €168,000 booked, €27,000 booked in the last 4 weeks (20 working days), 62% of linked tasks finished. Numbers are invented but internally consistent. Colours are approximate, not the app palette. Dashed tiles are new; the rest exist today.

Comparison

Both use contract money. The difference is what drives the forecast: recent spending pace (1) or progress-adjusted efficiency (2).

AspectOption 1 · AIPM burn-rateOption 2 · EVM formulas on price
Question answered When does the money run out at the current pace, and what will it have cost by the planned end? Given progress per euro so far, what will the whole scope cost?
Inputs Budget, bookings with dates, planned end, working-day calendar. Budget, bookings, % complete (linked tasks or manual), budget phased per period for PV.
This example EAC €261k · VAC −8.8% · runs out 26 Nov EAC €271k · VAC −12.9% · CPI 0.89 · SPI 0.85
Sees scope progress? No. A team burning steadily on the wrong work looks fine. Yes. Money spent without finished work lowers CPI and raises EAC.
Sees staffing changes? Yes, within 4 weeks: a new consultant raises the burn rate at once. Only indirectly, as CPI drifts.
Works without task links / % complete Yes. Needs bookings only. No. Without % complete, EV, CPI, ETC and EAC are all blank.
Early project (< 4 weeks booked) or pause Burn rate is thin or zero; forecast shown as “not enough recent bookings”. EV is tiny and CPI swings wildly in the first weeks.
Matches AIPM Project Burndown (§504) Yes, same formula, 4-week lookback, depletion date and “N weeks before project end” text. No. Different forecast; the AIPM dashboard has no EV or CPI.
EVM terminology (§501 ask) BAC, AC, ETC, EAC and VAC labels yes; ETC is a burn-rate ETC, not the PMI one (the hint says so). Full PMI set: BAC, PV, EV, AC, CPI, SPI, ETC, EAC, VAC.
Chart Burn-down with forecast line (extends today's chart). S-curve (new chart); EV only a point today, no curve until history is stored.
CPI confusion (§464) No new index. Existing effort indices relabelled “· effort”. Adds a price CPI next to the internal-cost “Cost recovery” and the effort CPI.
Explainable to a stakeholder “We spend €1,350 a day; €72k left lasts until 26 Nov.” Needs EV and CPI explained first.
Fixed-price buckets Budget is the contract amount; how actuals are valued still needs checking against consumedValue. Same check, plus % complete must exist on every budgeted bucket or the rollup blanks.
Build effort (estimate) M: dated booking series (exists for the burn-down), burn rate, depletion, 4 tiles, forecast line, labels. M–L: price-basis EV and CPI, PV per period, 9 tiles, new S-curve chart, disambiguating three CPIs.

A hybrid is possible: option 1 as the headline figures and forecast, with option 2's EAC shown as a second “EAC (performance-based)” only when % complete exists. That doubles the explanations needed; worth deciding after you've looked at both.
